Football Prediction Point System

Scoresonthedoors.uk is back with updated rules for this season. You can predict as many matches as you want. The standings will be determined by the highest average points. In case of a tie, the number of predicted results will be used as a tiebreaker (more predictions will result in a higher position in the standings).

We are starting with a test phase as usual. The first match of the season is the Community Shield, which will also mark a points reset and the official start of the season.

Currently, the scoring system remains unchanged, but we are open to suggestions for adjustments.

Prediction Type Points
Correct Full-Time Score 3
Correct Winning Team or Draw 1
Correct First Goal Scorer 3
Correct Anytime Goal Scorer (except the first goal) 1
